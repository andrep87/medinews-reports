New Targeted Drugs Reshape Second-Line Small Cell Lung Cancer Treatment

"Two B7-H3-targeted antibody-drug conjugates [drugs that deliver chemotherapy directly to cancer cells] showed significant survival benefits over standard chemotherapy in phase III trials in relapsed small cell lung cancer. A third B7-H3 candidate withdrew its FDA application after the agency found its phase 2 data insufficient. Both completed phase III trials were conducted in China."

Tambotatug pelitecan (tam-peli): A B7-H3-targeted antibody-drug conjugate. The phase III TAISHAN-302 trial in China (451 patients) showed median overall survival of 13.3 versus 9.4 months with topotecan chemotherapy (HR 0.46), progression-free survival 7.4 versus 2.8 months (HR 0.29), and response rate 59.1% versus 9.7%. Published in the New England Journal of Medicine (independent fact-check: the newsletter had identified this drug as DLL3-targeted and used an incorrect trial name; both are corrected).
Risvutatug rezetecan (ris-rez): Also a B7-H3-targeted antibody-drug conjugate. The phase III ARTEMIS-008 trial in China (461 patients) showed median overall survival of 18.5 versus 10.3 months with topotecan (HR 0.46) and response rate 58.3% versus 12.6%.
Ifinatamab deruxtecan (I-DXd): A third B7-H3-targeted ADC. The companies voluntarily withdrew their FDA application after agency feedback indicated the phase 2 data were insufficient. The phase III IDeate-Lung02 trial continues (audit correction: the newsletter stated the FDA withdrew the application).
Zocilurtatug pelitecan (zoci): A DLL3-targeted ADC. First global phase 1b/1c data are being presented, studying the drug in combination with atezolizumab with or without carboplatin in first-line extensive-stage SCLC.
Tarlatamab (Imdelltra): A DLL3-targeted bispecific T-cell engager [a drug that brings immune T cells into contact with cancer cells] — a different drug class from the ADCs above. Real-world data in 91 patients showed activity against brain metastases and acceptable safety alongside focused radiation.
Second-line SCLC options have been limited for decades, with topotecan chemotherapy delivering modest benefit. These trials show meaningfully longer survival with higher response rates.
B7-H3 is broadly expressed in SCLC, which is what makes it an attractive target — the antibody delivers a chemotherapy payload directly to cancer cells rather than throughout the body.
These drugs carry distinct toxicity profiles. Tambotatug pelitecan showed a lower overall rate of severe adverse events than topotecan but a higher risk of interstitial lung disease and pneumonitis [lung inflammation] (independent fact-check).
Having several agents in development across different targets may eventually give patients options if one approach fails (general medical guidance).
Patients with extensive-stage SCLC whose disease progressed on or after first-line platinum-etoposide chemotherapy, with or without immunotherapy.
Patients with relapsed SCLC, including those with brain metastases — both trials reported benefit extending to patients with brain involvement.
Important access limitation: both phase III trials were conducted entirely in China, and neither drug is approved outside that setting. Tarlatamab is the approved second-line option in the US.
Patients who may be candidates for first-line trials as these agents move earlier in treatment (general medical guidance).
If you have relapsed extensive-stage SCLC: ask your oncologist, 'What second-line options are available to me, and are there clinical trials of B7-H3-targeted drugs I could join?'
Understand the distinction between drug classes — tambotatug pelitecan and risvutatug rezetecan are B7-H3-targeted antibody-drug conjugates; tarlatamab is a DLL3-targeted bispecific T-cell engager. They work differently and have different side effects (independent fact-check).
If you have brain metastases: discuss whether the available options have shown activity in the brain, and ask about safety when combined with focused radiation.
Ask whether any trials of these agents are open at your center or nearby, particularly as they move into first-line settings (general medical guidance).

The Hope

  • Hope: Median survival of 13.3 and 18.5 months in these trials compares favorably with the roughly 9 to 10 months seen with topotecan — a meaningful gain in a disease with few options.
  • Reality: These are not cures. Resistance develops, and these drugs carry their own toxicities, including lung inflammation, that require specific monitoring.

The Reality

  • Reality: Access is the central limitation. Neither B7-H3 ADC is approved outside China. Global trials are underway but approval elsewhere will take time (general medical guidance).
  • Reality: Moving these drugs into first-line treatment adds complexity, and combination safety is still being established.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Tarlatamab (Imdelltra): A DLL3-targeted bispecific T-cell engager, FDA-approved for second-line extensive-stage SCLC. Being studied in first-line maintenance.

Tambotatug pelitecan (tam-peli, YL201): A B7-H3-targeted ADC with a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload. Positive phase III in China (TAISHAN-302); Roche is partnered for global development.

Risvutatug rezetecan (ris-rez): A B7-H3-targeted ADC, also with a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload. Positive phase III in China (ARTEMIS-008); GSK is partnered for global development.

Ifinatamab deruxtecan (I-DXd): A B7-H3-targeted ADC. BLA voluntarily withdrawn; phase III IDeate-Lung02 ongoing.

Zocilurtatug pelitecan (zoci): A DLL3-targeted ADC in phase 1b/1c, being tested in first-line combinations.

Evidence Uncertainty

Both positive phase III trials were conducted entirely in China, in populations with different demographics from Western patients. Generalizability is a genuine open question.

TAISHAN-302 was an open-label trial with investigator-assessed secondary endpoints, which can introduce bias even when the survival endpoint is objective.

Ifinatamab deruxtecan: The FDA determined the single-arm phase 2 data were insufficient for accelerated approval, and the companies withdrew the application. The randomized phase III trial is the appropriate next step.

Tarlatamab real-world data: 91 patients, retrospective, short follow-up, no control group.

Zocilurtatug pelitecan: Early-phase with small numbers; combination safety with chemotherapy and immunotherapy is not yet fully defined.

No head-to-head comparisons exist between these agents, and the optimal sequence is unknown.

What To Watch

Global phase III programs for tambotatug pelitecan (Roche) and risvutatug rezetecan (GSK) outside China.

IDeate-Lung02 results comparing ifinatamab deruxtecan with chemotherapy — the randomized data the FDA requested.

Whether DLL3-targeted treatment moves earlier into first-line maintenance.

Biomarker studies clarifying which patients benefit most from B7-H3 versus DLL3 targeting (general medical guidance).
