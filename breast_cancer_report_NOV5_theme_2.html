Next-Generation Antibody-Drug Conjugates Revolutionizing Care for HER2-Low, Triple-Negative, and Hormone Receptor-Positive Disease

"A wave of new antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) targeting HER2, TROP2, EGFR/HER3, and B7-H4 has demonstrated unprecedented efficacy in HER2-low, triple-negative, and hormone receptor-positive metastatic breast cancer, moving ADC therapy into first-line settings and creating an urgent need for biomarkers to match patients to the right drug."

HORIZON-Breast06: Trastuzumab rezetecan (HER2-directed ADC) showed median PFS 16.4 vs 6.8 months vs chemo in HER2-low metastatic breast cancer (HR 0.27), with 75.5% response rate.
APEX-Breast01: Biparatopic HER2 ADC (TQB2102/rolditamig deuderuxtecan) achieved median PFS 16.4 vs 5.5 months in HER2-low HR+ disease (HR 0.29), with dual-epitope binding design.
ASCENT-03/04: Sacituzumab govitecan (TROP2 ADC) approved June 2026 for first-line metastatic TNBC—monotherapy (PFS 9.7 vs 6.9 mo, HR 0.62) and +pembrolizumab for PD-L1 CPS≥10 (PFS 11.2 vs 7.8 mo, HR 0.65).
TROPION-Breast02: Datopotamab deruxtecan (TROP2 ADC) demonstrated OS benefit in first-line TNBC ineligible for immunotherapy (median OS 23.7 vs 18.7 mo, HR 0.79), supporting EU approval.
PANKU-Breast01/02: Izalontamab brengitecan (EGFR/HER3 bispecific ADC) showed PFS and OS improvements in previously treated TNBC (OS HR 0.60) and HR+/HER2- disease (PFS HR 0.41), a first for bispecific ADCs in breast cancer.
HER2-low disease (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-negative) now has multiple effective ADC options beyond trastuzumab deruxtecan, addressing ~50% of metastatic breast cancers previously without HER2-targeted therapy.
Triple-negative breast cancer—historically limited to chemotherapy—now has three distinct first-line ADC-based regimens (sacituzumab govitecan ± pembrolizumab, datopotamab deruxtecan) plus later-line options.
HR+/HER2- metastatic patients progressing on endocrine/CDK4/6 therapy now have TROP2 ADCs (datopotamab deruxtecan, datopotamab deruxtecan) and EGFR/HER3 ADC (izalontamab brengitecan) as chemotherapy alternatives.
The proliferation of ADCs with different targets (HER2, TROP2, EGFR/HER3, HER3, B7-H4) and payloads (topoisomerase I inhibitors, SN-38) creates treatment sequencing questions never before encountered.
HER2-low metastatic breast cancer (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-negative): Eligible for trastuzumab deruxtecan (if HR+, post-endocrine therapy), trastuzumab rezetecan, or TQB2102 depending on jurisdiction and prior treatment.
Metastatic triple-negative breast cancer: First-line options now include sacituzumab govitecan (if PD-L1 CPS<10 or immunotherapy-ineligible), sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab (if PD-L1 CPS≥10), or datopotamab deruxtecan (if immunotherapy-ineligible).
HR+/HER2- metastatic disease after endocrine therapy + CDK4/6 inhibitor: TROP2 ADCs and EGFR/HER3 bispecific ADC offer new chemotherapy-free pathways.
Patients with prior ADC exposure: Critical unanswered question—does switching target (e.g., HER2 to TROP2) or payload restore sensitivity after progression?
Biomarker testing for TROP2 or EGFR/HER3 is not currently required to receive these drugs, and no validated cutoffs exist, so routine testing is not recommended outside trials (general medical guidance).
For TNBC: Confirm PD-L1 CPS testing was done on a recent biopsy using an FDA-approved assay—this determines eligibility for sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab (CPS≥10) vs monotherapy or datopotamab deruxtecan.
For HR+/HER2-low: Verify you have progressed on endocrine therapy + CDK4/6 inhibitor before considering trastuzumab deruxtecan; ask if trastuzumab rezetecan or TQB2102 are available via trial or compassionate use.
You may wish to request discussion of treatment sequencing: "If I start ADC X now, what are my options at progression? Should I save a specific ADC for later based on my tumor's biomarkers?"

The Hope

  • Hope: Patients with historically treatment-resistant subtypes (TNBC, HR+ post-CDK4/6) now have multiple active ADC options with response rates 40-75% and PFS gains of roughly 3-5 months in triple-negative and about 4 months in HR-positive disease over chemo.
  • Reality: These are not cures—median PFS ranges 8-16 months; OS benefits are emerging but modest; most patients eventually progress.
  • Toxicity burden: Grade 3+ adverse events 60-85% across ADCs (neutropenia, diarrhea, nausea, stomatitis, ILD risk); quality of life impact significant during prolonged treatment.

The Reality

  • Cost and access: these are expensive therapies and insurance coverage varies; financial navigation support is worth asking about (general medical guidance).
  • Access disparities: Many agents approved in US/EU/China only; global access limited; clinical trials often exclude heavily pretreated or comorbid patients who need them most.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Trastuzumab rezetecan (SHR-A1811, Jiangsu Hengrui): HER2 ADC with topoisomerase I payload; phase III HORIZON-Breast06 positive; under regulatory review.

TQB2102/Rolditamig deuderuxtecan (Chia Tai Tianqing): Biparatopic HER2 ADC (dual epitope); phase III APEX-Breast01 positive; investigational.

Sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy, Gilead): TROP2 ADC with SN-38 payload; FDA-approved 1L TNBC (monotherapy & +pembrolizumab); ASCENT-07 OS update awaited for HR+ indication.

Datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd, Daiichi Sankyo/AstraZeneca): TROP2 ADC with topoisomerase I payload; EU-approved 1L TNBC (immunotherapy-ineligible); TROPION-Breast02 OS positive.

IZALONTAMAB BRENGITECAN (BL-B01D1, Boehringer Ingelheim): EGFR/HER3 bispecific ADC; phase III PANKU-Breast01 (HR+/HER2-) & 02 (TNBC) positive; first bispecific ADC with OS benefit in breast cancer.

B7-H4 ADC (AZD8205, AstraZeneca): Phase I/II BLUESTAR showed 20.5% ORR in B7-H4+ solid tumors including breast; early development.

Evidence Uncertainty

HORIZON-Breast06/APEX-Breast01: Comparator was chemotherapy, not trastuzumab deruxtecan; cross-trial comparisons invalid; OS immature; high grade 3+ toxicity (84% trastuzumab rezetecan).

ASCENT-03/04: OS immature at approval; ASCENT-07 (HR+ 1L) primary PFS negative—updated OS at SABCS 2026 (RF2-1) may clarify benefit.

TROPION-Breast02: OS benefit modest (HR 0.79); stomatitis/ocular toxicity distinct from sacituzumab govitecan; direct comparison lacking.

PANKU-Breast01/02: OS immature in HR+ cohort; EGFR/HER3 expression not validated as predictive biomarker; bispecific mechanism advantage unproven vs monospecific ADCs.

No head-to-head trials between competing ADCs; biomarker-guided selection remains investigational.

What To Watch

OptiTROP-Breast03 at SABCS 2026 (GS1-8): Sacituzumab tirumotecan vs chemo in 1L TNBC with PD-L1 CPS<10 or prior immunotherapy—could expand TROP2 ADC use further.

ASCENT-07 OS update (SABCS RF2-1): Will mature survival data rescue sacituzumab govitecan in 1L HR+/HER2- disease after negative PFS?

Direct ADC comparison trials: Urgently needed to guide sequencing (e.g., TROP2 vs HER2 ADC in HER2-low HR+ disease).

B7-H4, HER3, and novel target ADC development: BLUESTAR expansion, patritumab deruxtecan (HER3-DXd) CNS data, zanidatamab post-T-DXd.

Biomarker standardization: Quantitative HER2, TROP2, EGFR/HER3 assays; ctDNA for resistance monitoring; AI pathology for response prediction.
