Immunotherapy's Role in Breast Cancer Is Real but Narrow — Major Gains in Early TNBC, Disappointing in HR+/HER2- Metastatic Disease

"Immunotherapy (checkpoint inhibitors) has become standard for high-risk early triple-negative breast cancer, improving survival when added to chemotherapy before and after surgery. However, in hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer, adding pembrolizumab to chemotherapy provides only a tiny progression-free survival gain with no overall survival benefit and more side effects."

KEYNOTE-522 established pembrolizumab + chemo before surgery, then pembrolizumab after surgery, as standard for high-risk early TNBC: 5-year overall survival 86.6% vs 81.7% without immunotherapy.
KEYNOTE-B49 tested pembrolizumab + chemo in PD-L1-positive (CPS ≥1) HR+/HER2- metastatic BC: median PFS 6.7 vs 6.0 months (HR 0.74), but NO overall survival benefit (21.0 vs 22.2 months, HR 1.04). Higher PD-L1 (CPS ≥10) showed larger PFS gain (7.5 vs 4.4 months) but still no OS benefit.
POP-DURVA: Two doses of durvalumab (anti-PD-L1) before surgery in stage I TNBC induced major pathologic response in ~11% and a pathologic complete response in one patient, with immune remodeling in the tumor microenvironment.
ASCENT-04: Sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab improved PFS to 11.2 vs 7.8 months in untreated PD-L1+ advanced TNBC versus chemo + pembrolizumab.
Novel approaches: NECVAX-NEO1 (personalized neoantigen vaccine) showed 5/8 pathologic complete responses in neoadjuvant TNBC; Bria-IMT (whole-cell vaccine + retifanlimab) in Phase III for post-ADC metastatic BC.
In early TNBC, immunotherapy given before surgery (neoadjuvant) helps the immune system recognize and attack cancer cells when the tumor is still present, creating lasting immune memory that prevents recurrence.
In HR+/HER2- metastatic disease, tumors are "cold" (few immune cells, low mutation burden), so checkpoint inhibitors alone cannot activate enough immune response — the modest PFS gain likely reflects chemotherapy enhancement rather than true immunologic benefit.
PD-L1 testing (CPS score) helps select TNBC patients for immunotherapy but does not reliably identify HR+/HER2- patients who benefit.
Biomarkers like tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs), "MP High2" signature, and ER-low status may better predict immunotherapy response in early HR+/HER2- disease (per KEYNOTE-756 and CheckMate-7FL signals).
Early TNBC (stage II-III, or node-positive): Pembrolizumab + neoadjuvant chemo → surgery → adjuvant pembrolizumab is now standard of care.
Metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥10: Pembrolizumab + chemo is first-line standard; sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab is an emerging option.
Early HR+/HER2- BC: Immunotherapy NOT standard; investigational only in high-TIL, ER-low, or MP High2 subsets (trials like SWOG S2206 ongoing).
Metastatic HR+/HER2- BC: Pembrolizumab + chemo NOT recommended as standard (KEYNOTE-B49 negative for OS); prioritize CDK4/6i, ADCs, oral SERDs instead.
Heavily pretreated metastatic breast cancer: clinical trials of cancer vaccines (such as Bria-IMT) may be options; the NECVAX-NEO1 personalized vaccine is being studied in the neoadjuvant early-TNBC setting, not after ADCs (general medical guidance).
If newly diagnosed with early TNBC (stage II-III): Confirm your treatment plan includes pembrolizumab with neoadjuvant chemotherapy and adjuvant pembrolizumab for up to 1 year total.
If you have metastatic TNBC: Ask "What is my PD-L1 CPS score? Am I eligible for pembrolizumab + chemo first-line, or sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab?"
If you have HR+/HER2- metastatic BC: Do NOT expect immunotherapy to be recommended. Ask: "Why am I not getting immunotherapy? What are my best options — CDK4/6 inhibitor, ADC (Enhertu), oral SERD (elacestrant if ESR1-mutated), or PI3K/AKT/mTOR inhibitor?"
For early HR+/HER2- BC: Ask about clinical trials testing immunotherapy in high-TIL or ER-low subsets (e.g., SWOG S2206 for MP High2 signature).
Report any new cough, shortness of breath, diarrhea, rash, or thyroid symptoms immediately if on immunotherapy — these can signal immune-related adverse events needing steroids.

The Hope

  • Hope: Immunotherapy cures some early TNBC patients who would have recurred on chemo alone. Novel vaccines may eventually harness the immune system for durable control in metastatic settings.
  • Reality: In metastatic HR+/HER2- BC, immunotherapy adds toxicity without extending life. KEYNOTE-B49's 0.7-month PFS gain is not clinically meaningful for most patients.
  • Immune-related adverse events (colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies, pneumonitis) can be severe, permanent, or fatal — requiring lifelong hormone replacement in some cases.

The Reality

  • Vaccines (Bria-IMT, NECVAX-NEO1) are years from approval; manufacturing personalized vaccines is logistically complex and costly.
  • "Cold" tumors (most HR+/HER2-) fundamentally resist checkpoint inhibition; combinations with ADCs, radiation (SBRT), or targeted therapy needed to make them "hot."

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Pembrolizumab (Keytruda, Merck): anti-PD-1; approved for early high-risk TNBC (neoadjuvant plus adjuvant, KEYNOTE-522) and for first-line metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS >=10 plus chemotherapy (KEYNOTE-355, FDA-approved) (independent fact-check: the first-line metastatic TNBC approval is established; the audit flagged it only as absent from this week's sources).

Durvalumab (Imfinzi, AstraZeneca): Anti-PD-L1; POP-DURVA neoadjuvant window-of-opportunity study in stage I TNBC.

Sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy, Gilead): TROP2 ADC; ASCENT-04 testing combo with pembrolizumab in 1L metastatic TNBC.

NECVAX-NEO1 (NEC Bio): a personalized neoantigen vaccine combined with pembrolizumab and chemotherapy; Phase I/II in neoadjuvant TNBC, with 5 of 8 pathologic complete responses reported in an early cohort (general medical guidance).

Bria-IMT (BriaCell): Off-the-shelf GM-CSF-secreting whole-cell vaccine + retifanlimab (PD-1 inhibitor) + low-dose cyclophosphamide; Phase III Bria-ABC in post-ADC metastatic BC; FDA Fast Track.

Retifanlimab (Zynyz, Incyte): PD-1 inhibitor; partner for Bria-IMT.

Evidence Uncertainty

KEYNOTE-522: 1,174 patients; 5-yr OS 86.6% vs 81.7% (HR 0.72); pCR 64.8% vs 51.2%. Grade ≥3 immune-related AEs: 13.5% (vs 3.8% placebo). Long-term autoimmune sequelae unknown.

KEYNOTE-B49: 342 patients; PFS HR 0.74 (0.58-0.95); OS HR 1.04 (0.81-1.33). Protocol amended due to low PD-L1 prevalence; 99% had prior CDK4/6i. Grade ≥3 TRAEs 37.6% vs 32.7%; discontinuations 11.2% vs 5.4%.

POP-DURVA: 78 patients, single-arm Phase II; ~11% major pathologic response; 1 pCR. Hypothesis-generating only; no survival data.

NECVAX-NEO1: 8 patients, neoadjuvant; 5 pCR. Extremely early; no control arm; manufacturing complexity for personalized vaccines.

Bria-IMT: Phase II n=32, median 6 prior therapies; median OS 16.6 months, 27% alive at 2 years. Phase III Bria-ABC enrolling (target 404); interim analysis at 144 deaths.

What To Watch

SWOG S2206 results: Adjuvant pembrolizumab + chemo in MP High2 early HR+/HER2- BC (EFS endpoint).

KEYNOTE-756 / CheckMate-7FL final OS analyses in neoadjuvant HR+/HER2- BC (pCR gains ~10% but OS pending).

ASCENT-04 final OS in 1L metastatic TNBC (sacituzumab govitecan + pembro vs chemo + pembro).

Bria-ABC Phase III interim analysis (OS primary endpoint; first look projected 2026).

NECVAX-NEO1 expansion cohorts in US; Neo-CheckRay trial (SBRT + ICI to convert PD-L1-negative tumors "hot").

Biomarker refinement: TILs, TLS (tertiary lymphoid structures), MP High2, ER-low, ctDNA for immunotherapy patient selection.
