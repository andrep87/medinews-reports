Neoadjuvant Immunotherapy for MSS Colon Cancer: The ROBIN Trial, ctDNA Endpoints, and a Paradigm Shift

"For the first time, a Phase III trial (ROBIN) will test neoadjuvant immunotherapy (botensilimab + balstilimab) before surgery in high-risk MSS colon cancer—a population historically resistant to checkpoint inhibitors. The trial incorporates Signatera ctDNA testing as a key secondary endpoint, potentially establishing molecular response as a new measure of treatment success."

Natera and Agenus announced a partnership to embed Signatera ctDNA testing into the Phase III ROBIN trial, which will randomize high-risk stage II/III MSS (microsatellite-stable) colon cancer patients to neoadjuvant botensilimab (anti-CTLA-4) + balstilimab (anti-PD-1) followed by surgery vs. upfront surgery alone. (general medical guidance)
First patient enrollment expected Q1 2027; primary endpoint is event-free survival; key secondary endpoint is Signatera ctDNA negativity at 12 months. (general medical guidance)
This builds on NICHE-2 (neoadjuvant nivolumab + ipilimumab in dMMR colon cancer): 83% ctDNA clearance pre-surgery, 100% post-surgery, 3-year DFS 100%. (general medical guidance)
UNICORN showed activity in pMMR/MSS disease: 36% major/complete pathologic response with botensilimab + balstilimab; ctDNA clearance in all 5 pMMR responders tested. (general medical guidance)
NEST trial provided additional supportive evidence for the botensilimab/balstilimab combination. (general medical guidance)
Separately, a Chinese retrospective study (n=24, dMMR metastatic CRC) showed 47.6% pathologic complete response (pCR) and 62.6% total response rate with preoperative single-agent PD-1 blockade; 50% of T4b tumors avoided multivisceral resection.
MSS/pMMR colorectal cancer (the large majority of cases) has been essentially immunotherapy-resistant—extending benefit to this group would be the biggest advance in localized colon cancer in decades. (general medical guidance)
Neoadjuvant (pre-surgery) immunotherapy can shrink tumors, enable organ preservation (avoiding permanent stoma or multivisceral resection), and treat micrometastases early. (general medical guidance)
ctDNA (Signatera) as a trial endpoint could validate molecular response as a surrogate for long-term survival, accelerating future drug development and enabling ctDNA-guided treatment decisions (e.g., who needs adjuvant chemo). (general medical guidance)
The FOLR1 discovery (mirvetuximab soravtansine + anti-PD-1 restores response in MSS mouse models) identifies a combinatorial strategy to overcome primary resistance—FOLR1 stabilizes AARS1, lactylates STAT1, suppresses MHC-I and CD8+ T-cell engagement. (general medical guidance)
CEA-specific T-cell fold-change (measured by IFN-γ ELISpot) predicted anti-PD-1 response (AUC 0.866) and 12-month PFS (AUC 0.851) in dMMR/MSI-H patients—potential biomarker for patient selection.
ROBIN trial: Patients with high-risk stage II (T4, perforation, obstruction, poorly differentiated, <12 nodes, lymphovascular invasion) or stage III MSS/pMMR colon cancer who are surgical candidates—excludes dMMR/MSI-H (already have immunotherapy options). (general medical guidance)
Neoadjuvant immunotherapy generally: Patients with locally advanced rectal or colon cancer (especially T4b, bulky, threatening organ function) who want organ preservation—discuss with surgeon/oncologist before surgery. (general medical guidance)
dMMR/MSI-H metastatic: Patients eligible for preoperative PD-1 blockade per emerging data (e.g., Peking University protocol)—requires confirmed dMMR/MSI-H by IHC/PCR/NGS.
FOLR1-high tumors: Future trials may select for FOLR1 expression (associated with aggressive disease, poor ICB response)—testing not yet standard.
CEA-specific T-cell monitoring: Currently research-only; may apply to dMMR/MSI-H patients considering anti-PD-1 therapy once assay standardized.

The Hope

  • Hope: ROBIN could establish the first curative-intent immunotherapy for MSS colon cancer—changing "chemo forever" to "immunotherapy then maybe done." (general medical guidance)
  • Reality: Trial results years away (EFS primary endpoint); even if positive, access limited to trial sites initially; cost/toxicity of dual checkpoint blockade (grade 3 tox 8% in Peking study, higher in UNICORN). (general medical guidance)
  • Hope: ctDNA could spare patients unnecessary adjuvant chemo (in ctDNA-guided trials) and detect recurrence months earlier. (general medical guidance)
  • Reality: ctDNA false positives/negatives occur; not all insurers cover Signatera for adjuvant guidance; NCCN guidelines still evolving. (general medical guidance)

The Reality

  • Hope: FOLR1 targeting offers a rational combination to crack MSS resistance—MIRV already approved, repurposing feasible. (general medical guidance)
  • Reality: Mouse models often fail in humans; FOLR1 expression heterogeneous; ADC toxicity (ocular, neuropathy) adds to immunotherapy toxicity. (general medical guidance)
  • Hope: Organ preservation in T4b rectal tumors (50% avoided multivisceral resection in Peking study) transforms quality of life.
  • Reality: Emergency surgery risk during neoadjuvant therapy (17% in Peking); requires expert multidisciplinary team; not standard of care outside trials.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Botensilimab (anti-CTLA-4) + Balstilimab (anti-PD-1): Agenus investigational duo; ROBIN Phase III in MSS colon (neoadjuvant); UNICORN Phase II showed 36% major/complete pathologic response in pMMR. (general medical guidance)

Nivolumab + Ipilimumab: Standard in dMMR/MSI-H metastatic; NICHE-2 established neoadjuvant proof-of-concept in localized dMMR colon (100% 3-yr DFS).

Mirvetuximab soravtansine (MIRV, Elahere): FDA-approved ADC targeting FOLR1; preclinical data shows it restores anti-PD-1 sensitivity in MSS CRC by blocking FOLR1-AARS1-STAT1 axis—human trials needed. (general medical guidance)

Signatera (Natera): Tumor-informed ctDNA assay; integrated into ROBIN as secondary endpoint; already used in (general medical guidance)

CEA-specific T-cell ELISpot: Research assay (59 validated epitopes, covers >90% NE Asian HLA); predicts anti-PD-1 response in dMMR/MSI-H—commercialization pending.

Evidence Uncertainty

NICHE-2: dMMR only ; 100% 3-yr DFS impressive but small n, no control arm.

UNICORN/NEST: Single-arm Phase II; pMMR response rate 36% but durability unknown; no randomization.

Peking dMMR neoadjuvant: Retrospective, n=24, single-agent PD-1; 47.6% pCR but 4/24 needed emergency surgery (perforation/obstruction) during neoadjuvant phase.

FOLR1/MIRV: Mouse and in vitro only; MIRV approved for ovarian (FOLR1+), not CRC; human CRC FOLR1 prevalence and predictive value unknown.

CEA-T-cell: Derivation n=80, validation n=21 dMMR/MSI-H; AUC 0.81-0.87; needs prospective multi-center validation; assay not clinically available.

What To Watch

ROBIN trial launch: First patient Q1 2027; site activation announcements; interim safety/run-in data. (general medical guidance)

UNICORN/NEST long-term follow-up: 2-3 year DFS/OS in pMMR neoadjuvant botensilimab/balstilimab—critical for ROBIN powering. (general medical guidance)

Ongoing ctDNA-guided adjuvant trials will report whether de-escalation is safe. standard—OS endpoint. (general medical guidance)

FOLR1/MIRV CRC trials: Watch for Phase I/II combining MIRV + anti-PD-1 in MSS CRC (Agenus/ImmunoGen/Gilead). (general medical guidance)

CEA-T-cell assay: Commercialization efforts; prospective trial using assay to select anti-PD-1 therapy.

NCCN/ESMO guideline updates: Incorporation of neoadjuvant immunotherapy for dMMR colon; ctDNA adjuvant recommendations; organ preservation pathways. (general medical guidance)
