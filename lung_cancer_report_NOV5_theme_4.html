Antibody-Drug Conjugates and Bispecific Antibodies Advance in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Without Biomarkers to Guide Them

"A wave of phase 3 trials showed antibody-drug conjugates and bispecific antibodies improving outcomes in non-small cell lung cancer — targeting TROP2, HER3, c-MET and PD-1/VEGF. A central problem runs through them: most showed benefit without a validated test to identify who will respond, so patients are selected by tumour type and treatment history rather than by a biomarker."

OptiTROP-Lung06: Sacituzumab tirumotecan (TROP2 ADC) + pembrolizumab vs chemo+pembro in 1L PD-L1-negative nonsquamous NSCLC—median PFS 15.0 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.49); no TROP2 selection required. (general medical guidance)
OptiTROP-Lung04: Sacituzumab tirumotecan vs chemo post-EGFR TKI in EGFR-mut NSCLC—median OS 25.9 vs 17.4 months (HR 0.69); EGFR mutation = treatment-history biomarker, not TROP2 expression. (general medical guidance)
PANKU-Lung01: Izalontamab brengitecan (EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC) vs chemo post-3G EGFR TKI—median PFS 11.1 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.40); selected by EGFR mutation, not HER3 expression. (general medical guidance)
HARMONi-2: Ivonescimab (PD-1×VEGF bispecific) vs pembrolizumab in 1L PD-L1+ NSCLC—OS 30.8 vs 22.6 months (HR 0.73); PD-L1 eligibility, not VEGF biomarker. (general medical guidance)
c-MET ADC telisotuzumab vedotin: FDA accelerated approval for high c-MET protein (IHC 3+ ≥50%)—rare validated expression biomarker. (general medical guidance)
HER2: Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) approved for HER2 mutations (not amplification/overexpression); newer TKIs (zongertinib, sevabertinib) competing. (general medical guidance)
ADCs and bispecifics are transforming lung cancer treatment: delivering chemo payloads directly to tumors or redirecting immune cells, with unprecedented efficacy in resistant settings.
This means patients may receive expensive, toxic therapies without knowing if they'll benefit—or be denied access due to arbitrary biomarker thresholds. (general medical guidance)
The field needs biomarkers that reflect functional biology: antigen internalization, payload sensitivity, immune microenvironment, resistance mechanisms—not just static protein levels. (general medical guidance)
NSCLC with actionable alterations: HER2 mutation (T-DXd), EGFR mutation post-TKI (Dato-DXd, sac-TMT, iza-bren, ivonescimab+chemo), c-MET high protein (telisotuzumab vedotin), KRAS G12C (divarasib, adagrasib, sotorasib). (general medical guidance)
NSCLC without driver mutations: TROP2 ADCs (sac-TMT+pembro in PD-L1-neg), ivonescimab (PD-L1+), chemotherapy backbone. (general medical guidance)
BRAF V600E NSCLC: Encorafenib+binimetinib (PHAROS trial, approved EU/Singapore). (general medical guidance)
Germline testing candidates: Strong family history, young onset, multiple primaries—yield ~27% in selected vs 6% unselected.
Demand comprehensive NGS panel (DNA + RNA) at diagnosis and progression—covers EGFR, ALK, ROS1, RET, MET, HER2, KRAS, BRAF, NTRK, and emerging targets. (general medical guidance)
For germline: If family history of cancer, ask for referral to genetics counseling—hereditary mutations (EGFR, TP53, others) affect family screening and sometimes treatment. (general medical guidance)
Track your biomarker results in a personal health record—critical for future trial matching and treatment sequencing. (general medical guidance)

The Hope

  • These drugs extend life by months, not years—median OS gains 4-9 months in refractory settings; 1L gains larger but follow-up shorter.
  • Toxicity distinct: ADCs—ocular (corneal events), pneumonitis, neutropenia, neuropathy; Bispecifics—CRS, neurotoxicity (ICANS/TIAN), cytopenias. (general medical guidance)

The Reality

  • Sequencing unknown: Which ADC first? After TKI failure? With/after chemo? With immunotherapy? No head-to-head data.
  • Resistance inevitable: Antigen loss, payload resistance, bypass signaling—next-line options needed; trials testing combinations/sequences ongoing.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

TROP2: Dato-DXd (accel approval post-EGFR TKI), sac-TMT (OptiTROP-Lung06 1L, Lung04 2L), other TROP2 ADCs in development. (general medical guidance)

PD-1/VEGF: Ivonescimab (HARMONi-2 1L PD-L1+ NSCLC, HARMONi post-EGFR TKI PFS+ its prespecified primary overall-survival analysis did not meet statistical significance).

KRAS: Divarasib (Krascendo-1 vs sotorasib/adagrasib), daraxonrasib (pan-RAS, RASolve-301 vs docetaxel), G12D inhibitors.

Evidence Uncertainty

Biomarker validation lag: Only HER2 mutations and c-MET high protein have companion diagnostics; others use clinical/treatment-history enrichment. (general medical guidance)

Negative trial: SigVie-002 (integrin β6 ADC) failed OS despite early promise—shows target expression ≠ clinical benefit.

Spatial heterogeneity: Single biopsy may miss antigen expression; archival tissue may not reflect current tumor after multiple therapies.

Dynamic biomarkers: ctDNA clearance, on-treatment antigen modulation, immune profiling may outperform static IHC—prospective validation needed. (general medical guidance)

What To Watch

Combination studies: ADC + immunotherapy (OptiTROP-Lung06 model); bispecific + chemo (HARMONi); ADC + TKI; sequential ADC strategies.

KRAS inhibitor showdown: Divarasib vs adagrasib vs sotorasib in 1L + immunotherapy; pan-RAS vs mutant-selective.

Global access: China-first approvals (Tam-Peli, Ris-Rez, sac-TMT, iza-bren) vs US/EU timelines; biosimilar ADC development.
