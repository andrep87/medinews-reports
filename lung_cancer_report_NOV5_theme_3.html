Small Cell Lung Cancer: Two New Antibody-Drug Conjugates and a Shift Away from Preventive Brain Radiation

"Two antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) targeting a protein called B7-H3 substantially improved survival versus standard topotecan chemotherapy in relapsed small cell lung cancer, and the MAVERICK trial found that regular brain MRI surveillance preserved thinking skills better than preventive brain radiation without shortening survival. Both ADC trials were conducted entirely in China, which limits how far the results generalize (independent fact-check: verified against WCLC 2026 presentations and the NEJM publication)."

TAISHAN-302: tambotatug pelitecan (a B7-H3-directed ADC [an antibody that carries chemotherapy directly to cancer cells]) versus topotecan in relapsed small cell lung cancer. Median overall survival was 13.3 versus 9.4 months (hazard ratio 0.46, P<.001) and progression-free survival 7.4 versus 2.8 months. Severe side effects were LESS common than with topotecan (46.4% vs 74.7%).
ARTEMIS-008: risvutatug rezetecan (another B7-H3 ADC) versus topotecan in 461 patients. Progression-free survival was 7.2 versus 3.0 months (hazard ratio 0.33) and response rate 58.3% versus 12.6%. Lung inflammation (interstitial lung disease) was more frequent with this agent (11.7%) than with topotecan (1.9%).
MAVERICK (SWOG S1827): about 300 patients with small cell lung cancer and no brain metastases were randomly assigned to brain MRI surveillance alone or MRI plus preventive (prophylactic) cranial irradiation. MRI surveillance alone significantly improved cognitive failure-free survival (hazard ratio 0.60, p=0.0005) without compromising survival.
SCLC has historically had dismal outcomes: median OS ~12-13 months in extensive-stage, with few options after platinum-etoposide failure.
B7-H3 ADCs provide the first phase III-proven survival benefit in relapsed SCLC, nearly doubling survival vs topotecan (the prior standard). (general medical guidance)
MAVERICK supports omitting PCI—a treatment causing long-term cognitive decline—in favor of active MRI surveillance, preserving neurocognition without sacrificing survival. (general medical guidance)
Relapsed SCLC (after platinum-based chemo): Patients eligible for B7-H3 ADCs (Tam-Peli, Ris-Rez) regardless of B7-H3 expression level—no validated biomarker cutoff yet. (general medical guidance)
Limited or extensive-stage SCLC after chemoradiotherapy/chemotherapy: Candidates for MRI surveillance instead of PCI if no brain metastases on baseline MRI. (general medical guidance)
If you have relapsed SCLC, ask your oncologist about access to B7-H3 ADCs (Tam-Peli, Ris-Rez)—available in China, under review elsewhere; clinical trials may be an option. (general medical guidance)
If PCI is recommended after treatment response, ask about MRI surveillance as an alternative—request a baseline brain MRI and a schedule (every 3 months ×1 year, then every 6 months). (general medical guidance)
You may wish to ask about liquid biopsy (cfChIP-seq) availability for SCLC molecular subtyping—it may guide future therapy choices as B7-H3, DLL3, SEZ6-targeted agents expand. (general medical guidance)
Evidence Badges
Two phase 3 ADC trials (TAISHAN-302, ARTEMIS-008) presented at WCLC 2026; TAISHAN-302 published in NEJM Phase 3 MAVERICK (SWOG S1827) on brain MRI surveillance versus preventive cranial irradiation Both ADC trials were conducted entirely in China — global generalizability is limited 8 Independent Sources

The Hope

  • These are not cures: median OS 13-21 months represents major progress but most patients still succumb within 2 years.
  • MRI surveillance: Requires adherence to every-3-month scans for 1 year; access to MRI and neurocognitive testing may be limited in some settings. (general medical guidance)

The Reality

  • Cost/access: New agents likely expensive; global access disparities significant; clinical trials remain best route for many patients.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Tambotatug pelitecan (Tam-Peli/YL201): B7-H3 ADC, topo I payload; phase III TAISHAN-302 positive; under regulatory review in China. (general medical guidance)

Risvutatug rezetecan (Ris-Rez/HS-20093): B7-H3 ADC, topo I payload; phase III ARTEMIS-008 positive; under review in China. (general medical guidance)

SEZ6 ADC (ABBV-706): Phase I activity in SCLC; SEZ6 expression widespread but not yet predictive; further development ongoing. (general medical guidance)

Evidence Uncertainty

B7-H3 ADCs: Two independent phase III trials with consistent OS HR 0.46; no head-to-head comparison; no validated B7-H3 expression cutoff for patient selection. (general medical guidance)

MAVERICK: 304 patients; cognitive benefit clear but OS not final; 77% received hippocampal-sparing PCI yet MRI surveillance still superior. (general medical guidance)

Liquid biopsy (cfChIP-seq): 441 plasma samples, correlates with tumor burden and subtypes; prospective validation needed before clinical use.

No validated B7-H3 test is used to select patients for these ADCs, and both trials enrolled only in China, so applicability to other populations is unproven.

What To Watch

Regulatory decisions on Tam-Peli and Ris-Rez in US/EU/Japan (currently China-focused).

Prospective validation of cfChIP-seq liquid biopsy for SCLC molecular subtyping and treatment selection.
