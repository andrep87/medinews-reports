Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs) Rapidly Reshaping Lung Cancer Treatment

"Antibody-drug conjugates are moving from late-line rescue therapies toward first-line backbones in both NSCLC and SCLC, with 10 distinct programs targeting TROP2, HER3, c-MET, B7-H3, DLL3, and bispecific antigens at ESMO 2026."

ESMO 2026 features roughly 10 lung cancer antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) [targeted drugs that link an antibody to a cancer-killing chemotherapy payload, delivering it directly to tumor cells] across NSCLC and SCLC, testing targets such as TROP2, HER3, c-MET, B7-H3, and DLL3, plus bispecific formats.
Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT, TROP2 ADC) in two phase III trials: OptiTROP-Lung06 (1L + pembro vs chemo + pembro in PD-L1-negative nonsquamous) and OptiTROP-Lung04 (post-EGFR TKI vs platinum chemo, final OS).
Izalontamab brengitecan (iza-bren, EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC) in PANKU-Lung01 phase III post-third-gen EGFR TKI; earlier data show activity in heavily pretreated EGFR-mutant disease.
Ruzaltatug rezetecan (HER3 ADC) in HERON-Lung01 phase III vs platinum chemo post-EGFR TKI; HER3 targeting may work across multiple resistance mechanisms.
Telisotuzumab adizutecan (c-MET ADC) presents first-in-human OS data; exploits MET protein expression regardless of signaling dependence.
SCLC: B7-H3 ADCs (risvutatug rezetecan, ifinatamab deruxtecan, tambotatug pelitecan) and DLL3 ADCs (zocilurtatug pelitecan, SHR-4849) in phase III/competitive development; tarlatamab (DLL3 T-cell engager) already approved.
ADCs deliver potent chemotherapy payloads directly to cancer cells via surface antigens, bypassing the need for oncogene addiction — they work even when the target is not the primary driver.
This is transformative for post-TKI resistance where multiple heterogeneous clones emerge; targeting a surface protein (HER3, TROP2, c-MET, B7-H3) may control diverse resistant populations.
Moving ADCs earlier (1L NSCLC, 1L SCLC) could replace or enhance chemotherapy backbones, improving therapeutic index.
Bispecific ADCs (EGFR×HER3) engage two antigens, potentially overcoming antigen heterogeneity and loss.
SCLC has had no new targets for decades; DLL3 (tarlatamab) and B7-H3 now offer two distinct ADC/engager classes with survival benefits.
NSCLC patients with EGFR mutations progressing on third-generation TKIs (osimertinib, lazertinib, aumolertinib) — eligible for PANKU-Lung01 (iza-bren), HERON-Lung01 (ruzatatug rezetecan), OptiTROP-Lung04 (sac-TMT), or TROP2 ADC datopotamab deruxtecan.
NSCLC patients without driver mutations (PD-L1-negative nonsquamous) — OptiTROP-Lung06 tests sac-TMT + pembro vs chemo + pembro in 1L.
SCLC patients with extensive-stage disease relapsed after platinum chemo — multiple B7-H3 and DLL3 ADC trials (EMBOLD-SCLC-301, IDeate-Lung02, TAISHAN-302, DeLLphi-304).
Patients with c-MET overexpression or MET exon 14 skipping — telisotuzumab vedotin (Teliso-V) shows activity in EGFR wild-type MET-high disease.
If progressing on an EGFR TKI, you may wish to ask: "Which ADC trials or approved options match my resistance profile, and is antigen expression testing (HER3, TROP2, c-MET) available?" (general medical guidance).
For EGFR exon 20 insertion, you may wish to ask about standard first-line options and post-progression ADC trials (general medical guidance).
For SCLC relapsed after platinum chemotherapy, you may wish to ask about tarlatamab (an approved DLL3-directed therapy) and B7-H3 or DLL3 ADC trials (general medical guidance).
You may wish to ask about tumor antigen-expression testing if considering an ADC trial (general medical guidance).
You may wish to discuss ILD/pneumonitis [lung inflammation] risk with ADCs and ask about baseline lung-function testing and a monitoring plan (general medical guidance).
Evidence Badges
Multiple Phase III ADC trials featured at ESMO 2026 (readouts pending) FDA approval: tarlatamab for SCLC Regulatory note: ifinatamab deruxtecan US BLA withdrawn (2026) 7 Independent Sources

The Hope

  • Hope: ADCs bring chemotherapy precision — higher tumor delivery, less systemic toxicity; multiple targets create options for resistant disease; SCLC finally has targeted options beyond chemo.
  • Reality: ILD/pneumonitis is a recognized class concern for several ADCs (rates vary by agent), and hematologic toxicity (such as low blood counts) is common with topoisomerase-I payloads (general medical guidance).
  • Most phase III trials use chemotherapy controls (topotecan, docetaxel, platinum) — not modern standards (tarlatamab, chemoimmunotherapy); cross-trial comparisons unreliable.

The Reality

  • Cost: ADCs are expensive biologics + payloads; IV administration every 3 weeks; combination with immunotherapy adds further cost/complexity.
  • Antigen heterogeneity: B7-H3 expression variable; some trials don't require biomarker positivity — may dilute benefit in low expressors.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT, SKB264/MK-2870, Kelun/Merck): a TROP2 ADC in multiple lung cancer trials (OptiTROP-Lung program) (general medical guidance).

Izalontamab brengitecan (iza-bren, BL-B01D1): an EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC in Phase III (PANKU-Lung01) after third-generation EGFR TKI, with activity reported in pretreated disease (general medical guidance).

Ruzaltatug rezetecan (SHR-A2009, Hengrui): a HER3 ADC in Phase III (HERON-Lung01) versus platinum chemotherapy after EGFR TKI (general medical guidance).

Telisotuzumab adizutecan and telisotuzumab vedotin (AbbVie): c-MET-directed ADCs for MET-overexpressing disease (general medical guidance).

B7-H3 ADCs (such as risvutatug rezetecan, ifinatamab deruxtecan, tambotatug pelitecan): in Phase III development for SCLC; ifinatamab deruxtecan's US BLA was withdrawn in 2026 while its global Phase III continues (general medical guidance).

DLL3-directed therapies: tarlatamab (an approved T-cell engager) plus DLL3 ADCs (such as zocilurtatug pelitecan) in development (general medical guidance).

Datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd, Datroway): a TROP2 ADC with reported activity in EGFR-mutant NSCLC after TKI (general medical guidance).

Evidence Uncertainty

Several SCLC ADC trials report survival benefits versus topotecan in randomized studies, though cross-trial comparisons are unreliable (general medical guidance).

The TROP2 ADC sac-TMT showed benefit when added to pembrolizumab in one trial, but interpreting the result depends on the control arm used (general medical guidance).

One integrin-β6 ADC (sigvotatug vedotin) was negative overall in its Phase III, suggesting target or line-of-therapy selection matters (general medical guidance).

Ifinatamab deruxtecan's US accelerated-approval application was withdrawn after the FDA deemed the Phase II data insufficient; a global Phase III continues.

Tarlatamab plus durvalumab as first-line maintenance in extensive-stage SCLC was reported positive (DeLLphi-304); full data are awaited (independent fact-check: corrected from the draft's 'DeLLphi-305').

Key unknowns: head-to-head ADC comparisons, optimal sequencing after ADC failure, and differences in ILD risk across payloads (general medical guidance).

What To Watch

ESMO 2026 readouts: OptiTROP-Lung04 final OS, OptiTROP-Lung06 PFS/OS, HERON-Lung01 interim, PANKU-Lung01, SigVie-002 subgroup, EMBOLD PanTumour-101 dose optimization.

DeLLphi-304 full publication: Tarlatamab + durvalumab 1L maintenance ES-SCLC (press release positive).

IDeate-Lung02 (I-DXd vs chemo 2L+ SCLC) and EMBOLD-SCLC-301 (risvutatug rezetecan 2L+ SCLC) phase III results (2027).

Tambotatug pelitecan NSCLC expansion data and global phase III planning.

Zocilurtatug pelitecan 1L SCLC combination maturity; SHR-4849 phase II durability.

Datopotamab deruxtecan TROPION-Lung07/08/10 in 1L NSCLC (with/without chemo/immunotherapy).

Biomarker studies: Does HER3, TROP2, c-MET, B7-H3, or DLL3 expression predict ADC benefit?
