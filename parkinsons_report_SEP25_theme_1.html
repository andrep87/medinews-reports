Emerging Disease-Modifying Therapies and Novel Treatment Delivery Systems

"Multiple experimental therapies are advancing toward clinical testing, targeting Parkinson's root causes rather than just symptoms. These include a wearable-delivered long-acting apomorphine [a dopamine-stimulating drug] (SER-252), a dual-target oral drug blocking LRRK2 and c-Abl [two enzymes linked to Parkinson's nerve cell damage] (1ST-104), sleep-enhancing compounds to clear toxic proteins, and a specialized acupuncture technique showing measurable motor and quality-of-life benefits in a randomized trial."

Serina Therapeutics' SER-252, a long-acting apomorphine delivered via the Enfuse wearable injector, cleared its first safety review in a Phase 1b trial (NCT07422675) and advanced to a second dosing cohort, with top-line data expected in first half of 2027.
SK Biopharmaceuticals acquired global rights to 1ST Biotherapeutics' 1ST-104, a dual LRRK2/c-Abl inhibitor, in a deal worth up to $314.8 million; 1ST-104 is a Type 2 inhibitor that locks both enzymes in inactive conformations and crosses the blood-brain barrier in preclinical models.
AstronauTx received funding from The Michael J. Fox Foundation and Parkinson's UK to develop sleep-enhancing compounds that boost restorative sleep (slow-wave and REM) to enhance glymphatic clearance of alpha-synuclein, with lead program targeting clinical development in 2027.
A randomized, sham-controlled trial of Qihuang needle (QHN) acupuncture in 140 patients showed clinically meaningful improvements in motor function (UPDRS-III), nonmotor symptoms (NMSS), and quality of life (PDQ-39) at 6 and 10 weeks, with benefits greatest in postural instability/gait-dominant subtype.
Current Parkinson's treatments only replace dopamine or stimulate its receptors; they do not slow the underlying nerve cell loss. These new approaches target disease mechanisms: LRRK2 overactivity, c-Abl–mediated alpha-synuclein toxicity, impaired protein clearance during sleep, and neural circuit modulation via acupuncture.
SER-252's wearable delivery could replace burdensome frequent injections or bulky infusion pumps, offering sustained apomorphine exposure with weekly subcutaneous dosing—potentially reducing "off" time and dyskinesia from levodopa fluctuations.
Sleep-based therapies aim to intervene at the prodromal stage (before motor symptoms), using REM sleep behavior disorder as an entry biomarker—representing a shift toward true disease modification rather than symptomatic relief.
Qihuang needle acupuncture provides a non-pharmacologic adjunct that improves both motor and nonmotor symptoms without adding drug side effects, addressing a major gap in long-term dopaminergic therapy complications.
SER-252: Patients with advanced Parkinson's experiencing motor fluctuations ("off" episodes, dyskinesia) despite optimized oral medications; trial enrolls adults withdrawing from background Parkinson's drugs to isolate SER-252 effects.
1ST-104: Initially targets genetically defined subgroups with LRRK2 mutations (among the most common genetic causes), but preclinical blood-brain barrier penetration suggests broader sporadic Parkinson's potential.
Sleep-based therapies: Individuals with REM sleep behavior disorder (RBD) or other prodromal markers—often years before motor diagnosis—who could receive disease-modifying treatment at the earliest biological stage.
Qihuang needle acupuncture: Patients with idiopathic Parkinson's (Hoehn & Yahr stages 1–4) on stable medication regimens, particularly those with postural instability/gait-dominant phenotype who showed the greatest motor benefit.
Ask your movement disorder specialist whether you qualify for the SER-252 Phase 1b trial (NCT07422675) at U.S. or Australian sites, especially if you have advanced disease with motor fluctuations and are willing to pause other Parkinson's medications temporarily.
If you have a known LRRK2 mutation or strong family history, discuss 1ST-104 development timeline with your genetic counselor or neurologist; ask about LITE consortium (LRRK2 Investigative Therapeutics Exchange) updates supported by MJFF.
If you experience vivid dreams with acting out (punching, kicking, shouting during sleep), request a sleep study for REM sleep behavior disorder evaluation—this may qualify you for future prodromal prevention trials like AstronauTx's program.
Inquire about integrating acupuncture with a practitioner trained in Qihuang needle technique (hollow oval-tipped needle, 3–5 acupoints, strong Deqi stimulation) as an adjunct to your current regimen; verify insurance coverage for 9 sessions over 6 weeks.

The Hope

  • SER-252 offers hope for convenient weekly injection replacing daily rescue doses or pump therapy, but apomorphine itself can cause nausea, low blood pressure on standing, and injection-site reactions — these are known class effects (general medical guidance).
  • 1ST-104's dual mechanism is scientifically promising, but earlier LRRK2 inhibitors caused lung-related side effects (Type 1 inhibitors); 1ST-104 uses a different binding approach (Type 2) designed to avoid this. The $300M deal reflects commercial confidence, but years of clinical trials are needed before potential approval.

The Reality

  • Sleep modification as disease prevention is a paradigm shift — if it works, it could delay onset significantly — but translating sleep-based protein clearance from animal models to humans is unproven, and trials would need to be very long (general medical guidance).
  • Acupuncture provides symptom relief without drug side effects, but access to Qihuang-trained practitioners may be limited outside specialized centers. This is not a disease-modifying therapy (general medical guidance).

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

SER-252 (POZ-apomorphine, Serina Therapeutics): Long-acting subcutaneous apomorphine via Enfuse wearable; Phase 1b registrational trial under FDA 505(b)(2) pathway; uses existing apomorphine safety data; $30M recent funding.

1ST-104 (1ST Biotherapeutics/SK Biopharmaceuticals): Oral dual LRRK2/c-Abl inhibitor [blocks two enzymes involved in nerve cell damage]; preclinical data show brain penetration and target selectivity. SK acquired global rights in a deal worth up to $314.8 million. Phase 1 in healthy volunteers completed; further development planned.

AstronauTx sleep-enhancing compounds (AstronauTx Ltd): Novel molecules boosting slow-wave sleep and REM restorative mechanisms; MJFF-funded preclinical work on alpha-synuclein clearance; Parkinson's UK-funded UCL glymphatic flow study; clinical development planned 2027.

Qihuang Needle Therapy (Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine): Specialized acupuncture with hollow oval-tipped needle; 9 sessions over 6 weeks; RCT published in Movement Disorders 2026 (n=140); minimal adverse events (needle-site bleeding/hematoma only).

Evidence Uncertainty

SER-252: First 8-patient cohort cleared safety review; sustained apomorphine exposure matching target profile. The trial is randomized and placebo-controlled with exploratory motor endpoints (MDS-UPDRS Parts 2/3 [standardized Parkinson's motor and daily-living assessments]). Long-term safety and wearability are unknown.

1ST-104: Preclinical data show target engagement and BBB crossing; Type 2 binding may avoid pulmonary toxicity seen with Type 1 LRRK2 inhibitors; but no human efficacy data yet—Phase 1 only tested single ascending doses in healthy volunteers.

Sleep-based approach: Strong mechanistic rationale (glymphatic clearance peaks during slow-wave sleep; alpha-synuclein accumulates when sleep disrupted); but compounds are preclinical—no human pharmacokinetics, safety, or biomarker data yet.

Qihuang needle: RCT showed 8.45-point UPDRS-III between-group difference at week 6 (exceeds minimal clinically important difference), widening to 10.88 at week 10; limitations include single-center design, unblinded acupuncturists, 10-week follow-up only, and higher control-group dropout.

What To Watch

SER-252 top-line Phase 1b data (expected first half 2027); potential advancement toward further trials.

1ST-104 further clinical development — Phase 1 in patients is the next expected step.

AstronauTx lead compound progress toward clinical development (planned 2027); preclinical data on protein clearance during sleep.

Whether larger, multicenter Qihuang needle trials with longer follow-up and brain imaging confirm the initial findings.
