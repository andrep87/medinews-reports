New First-Line Option in Development for RAS-Mutated Metastatic Colorectal Cancer: Onvansertib Earns FDA Fast Track

"Multiple innovative strategies are advancing for treatment-refractory metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC): Onvansertib (PLK1 inhibitor) earned FDA Fast Track with a 72% response rate in RAS-mutated first-line disease; City of Hope launched a bispecific immunotherapy trial (ivonescimab + ADG126); a curcumin analogue (FLLL32) triggers ferroptosis in resistant models; and highly selected patients with liver-only metastases are achieving long-term survival after liver transplant."

Onvansertib (an oral PLK1 inhibitor from Cardiff Oncology) with FOLFIRI and bevacizumab received FDA Fast Track designation on 16 November 2026 for first-line RAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer, based on the randomised PHASE 2 CRDF-004 trial (110 patients), where the 30 mg dose achieved a 72.2% response rate. A registrational phase 3 trial is planned for 2027 (independent fact-check: the draft described this as a Phase III fast-track with enrolment beginning in 2027; CRDF-004 is phase 2, and the phase 3 is only planned). (general medical guidance)
Ivonescimab + ADG126 (muzastotug): City of Hope/Adagene Phase I trial (n=36) for advanced MSS mCRC. Ivonescimab = PD-1/VEGF bispecific; ADG126 = CTLA-4 bispecific (tumor-activated). Combo + chemo (5-FU → +irinotecan) planned as maintenance then 1L. (general medical guidance)
FLLL32 (curcumin analogue): Synthetic STAT3 inhibitor triggers ferroptosis (iron-dependent lipid peroxidation death) in CRC organoids and xenografts. IC50 0.33-0.44 µM; suppresses pSTAT3 → downregulates GPX4 (lipid peroxide detox) and SLC40A1 (iron export) → lethal iron/ROS accumulation. Wide therapeutic window (spares normal intestinal cells at 5 µM).
Mirvetuximab soravtansine (MIRV, anti-FOLR1 ADC): Preclinical (mouse/in vitro) shows FOLR1 drives immunotherapy resistance via AARS1-STAT1-MHC-I axis; MIRV + anti-PD-1 restores CD8+ T-cell killing in MSS CRC models. MIRV FDA-approved for ovarian cancer.
Liver transplant for mCRC: Mayo Clinic performed first liver transplant for colon cancer with liver-only metastases (Shannon Jones, cancer-free 2 years). Iowa patient also cancer-free post-transplant. Criteria: liver-only disease, no extrahepatic spread, primary tumor controlled, response to systemic therapy.
Patient stories: Multiple stage IV patients (Bianca Harvey 3.5 years, Winston-Salem father, Iowa dad) achieving long-term remission via clinical trials, transplant, or novel combinations—real-world proof of progress.
RAS-mutated mCRC (50-60% of cases): No targeted therapy exists; standard 1L is chemo + bevacizumab (ORR ~40%, PFS ~11 mo). Onvansertib's 30% ORR improvement is unprecedented—targets PLK1 (mitotic kinase) to enhance chemo efficacy independent of RAS status. (general medical guidance)
MSS mCRC (85% of cases): Immunotherapy fails; bispecifics (ivonescimab PD-1/VEGF, ADG126 CTLA-4) engage T-cells and normalize vasculature simultaneously—rational combo for "cold" tumors. (general medical guidance)
Ferroptosis (FLLL32): Bypasses apoptosis resistance (common in CRC); targets STAT3-GPX4/SLC40A1 axis—novel death mechanism for refractory tumors; curcumin derivative solves bioavailability issues.
FOLR1/MIRV: Identifies a druggable resistance mechanism; MIRV already approved—rapid repurposing path for MSS CRC immunotherapy combinations.
Liver transplantation is being explored for highly selected patients with liver-only metastatic colorectal cancer; the sources do not provide survival figures, so specific outcomes should not be assumed (general medical guidance).

The Hope

  • Hope: Onvansertib could become 1L standard for RAS-mut mCRC—oral, chemo-enhancing, RAS-agnostic. (general medical guidance)
  • Hope: Bispecifics (ivonescimab/ADG126) could "heat up" MSS tumors—dual immune/angiogenic attack.
  • Reality: CTLA-4 toxicity (colitis, hepatitis) + PD-1 + VEGF (hypertension, bleeding, proteinuria) = high adverse event burden; tumor-activated ADG126 unproven in humans.
  • Hope: Ferroptosis (FLLL32) kills resistant cells via new mechanism—bypasses BCL2, p53, apoptosis defects.
  • Reality: Iron metabolism targeting risks anemia, organ iron overload; STAT3 inhibition may affect normal immunity/regeneration; curcumin analogues have failed before.

The Reality

  • Hope: Liver transplant offers cure for some stage IV—Jones cancer-free 2 years, back coaching.
  • Reality: <1% of mCRC patients meet criteria; lifelong immunosuppression; donor shortage; only at few centers; cost ~$1M+; not standard of care.
  • Hope: Oral care bundle cuts surgical infections in half—simple, cheap, scalable.
  • Reality: Not standard everywhere; periodontitis-specific effect unproven; dental access barriers for many.

Ivonescimab (Akeso/Adagene): PD-1/VEGF bispecific; 82% ORR in small MSS CRC trial (n=22) with chemo; China approval for NSCLC. (general medical guidance)

ADG126/Muzastotug (Adagene): Masked CTLA-4 bispecific; activates in tumor microenvironment (low pH); 3rd clinical program combining with PD-1 bispecific. (general medical guidance)

FLLL32 (Wu et al., Mol Biomed 2026): STAT3-targeted ferroptosis inducer; patient-derived organoid screening hit; xenograft efficacy 50mg/kg IP daily; no overt toxicity.

Mirvetuximab soravtansine (ImmunoGen/Gilead): FOLR1 ADC; approved ovarian; preclinical CRC combo with anti-PD-1; FOLR1 IHC assay development needed.

MIRV/FOLR1: Mouse syngeneic (CT26, MC38), in vitro human CRC lines; FOLR1-AARS1-STAT1 axis mechanistically detailed; human FOLR1 prevalence in MSS CRC not defined.

Liver transplant: Case reports/series (Jones, Iowa, literature); selection bias extreme; 5-yr OS on chemo 9% (Harnois); transplant series small; immunosuppression recurrence risk theoretical.

SCN4A neuropathy: Japanese prospective (n=76); rs2302237 protective for chronic neuropathy (B -4.4 to -5.2, p=0.009-0.016); exploratory, no multiplicity correction; European studies showed opposite effect.
