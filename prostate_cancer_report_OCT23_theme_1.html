ESMO 2026: Major Trial Readouts Reshaping Prostate Cancer Care Across All Stages

"The ESMO 2026 Congress will present practice-changing data from multiple phase III trials spanning localized, hormone-sensitive, castration-resistant, and neuroendocrine prostate cancer. These readouts will clarify whether adding newer drugs to standard therapy improves survival and quality of life, and how liquid biopsies can guide treatment selection."

ESMO 2026 will feature 10 high-impact prostate cancer presentations, including interim and final analyses from four major phase III trials: DASL-HiCaP, CAPItello-281, TALAPRO-3, and PROTEUS.
The DASL-HiCaP trial will report an interim analysis testing whether adding darolutamide (an androgen receptor inhibitor, a drug that blocks the hormone signal prostate cancer depends on) to standard androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) [treatment that lowers testosterone] plus radiation improves metastasis-free survival in very high-risk localized prostate cancer.
CAPItello-281 will present updated overall survival data for capivasertib (an AKT inhibitor) plus abiraterone/prednisone in PTEN-deficient metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer; the FDA approved this combination in June 2026 based on progression-free survival benefit.
TALAPRO-3 will share two complementary analyses: circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) tumor fraction as a prognostic biomarker, and patient-reported quality-of-life outcomes from the talazoparib (PARP inhibitor) plus enzalutamide regimen in HRR gene-mutated metastatic hormone-sensitive disease.
These trials address the most pressing treatment questions across the disease spectrum: intensifying therapy for high-risk localized cancer, optimizing triplet regimens for metastatic hormone-sensitive disease, and refining PARP inhibitor use through better biomarkers.
If DASL-HiCaP is positive, darolutamide could become a new standard addition to radiation for very high-risk localized patients, aiming to improve long-term, metastasis-free outcomes (general medical guidance).
CAPItello-281's updated overall survival data will determine whether the PFS benefit of capivasertib translates into longer life, which is the gold standard for regulatory and clinical decisions.
TALAPRO-3's ctDNA analysis could replace invasive tissue biopsies for monitoring treatment response and detecting resistance early, while patient-reported outcomes reveal whether the PFS gain comes with acceptable quality of life.
Men with very high-risk localized prostate cancer (high Gleason score, high PSA, or advanced local stage) who are candidates for definitive radiation therapy may be affected by DASL-HiCaP results.
Men newly diagnosed with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer whose tumors have PTEN loss (tested on tissue or liquid biopsy) are the target population for capivasertib plus abiraterone.
Men with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer harboring homologous recombination repair (HRR) gene mutations (like BRCA2, ATM) are the population for talazoparib plus enzalutamide.
Men with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) undergoing liquid biopsy for PARP inhibitor eligibility need the clonal hematopoiesis analysis to avoid false-positive HRR results.
You may wish to ask your oncologist whether your cancer has been tested for PTEN loss and HRR gene mutations (such as BRCA1/2 or ATM), and whether you qualify for any of the combination regimens being discussed at ESMO (general medical guidance).
If you have metastatic hormone-sensitive disease and have not had genomic testing, you may wish to ask about both tissue and liquid biopsy (ctDNA) profiling to identify treatment targets (general medical guidance).
If you are considering radiation for high-risk localized disease, you may wish to ask your radiation oncologist whether a clinical trial adding an androgen receptor inhibitor (such as darolutamide) is available (general medical guidance).
If you have mCRPC and a liquid biopsy shows an HRR mutation, you may wish to ask whether it could be clonal hematopoiesis (a benign age-related blood change) rather than a tumor mutation, and whether confirmatory tissue testing is appropriate (general medical guidance).

The Hope

  • Hope: Multiple phase III readouts in one conference could redefine standards across all disease stages within 12–24 months.
  • Reality: Interim analyses (DASL-HiCaP) and immature OS data (CAPItello-281, TALAPRO-3) mean definitive practice changes may wait for final results.

The Reality

  • Hope: Liquid biopsy (ctDNA) could spare men repeat invasive biopsies and enable real-time treatment monitoring.
  • Reality: Clonal hematopoiesis confounds liquid biopsy interpretation; tissue confirmation still often needed; assays not yet standardized across labs.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Darolutamide (Nubeqa) plus ADT and radiation: Phase III DASL-HiCaP interim analysis at ESMO 2026; targets very high-risk localized disease; primary endpoint metastasis-free survival.

Capivasertib (Truqap) plus abiraterone/prednisone: FDA approved June 2026 for PTEN-deficient mHSPC; EMA application withdrawn by AstraZeneca; updated OS at ESMO 2026 (independent fact-check: FDA approval June 12, 2026 and the CAPItello-281 design verified against primary sources).

Talazoparib (Talzenna) plus enzalutamide (Xtandi): Phase III TALAPRO-3 showed rPFS benefit (HR 0.48) in HRR-mutated mHSPC; ctDNA and QoL analyses at ESMO 2026.

Apalutamide (Erleada) perioperative: PROTEUS substudy compares apalutamide + ADT + surgery vs surgery alone in high-risk localized disease; primary analysis showed improved pathologic complete response and MFS.

Evidence Uncertainty

DASL-HiCaP: an interim analysis; final metastasis-free survival and overall survival data are not yet mature (the trial was designed for about 1,100 patients) (general medical guidance).

CAPItello-281: Primary PFS positive (HR 0.81, P=0.034); interim OS not significant (HR 0.90, 26% maturity); updated OS at ESMO 2026 still may not reach statistical significance.

TALAPRO-3: rPFS strongly positive (HR 0.48, P<0.0001); interim OS not significant (HR 0.77); ctDNA tumor fraction and QoL are exploratory endpoints.

Clonal hematopoiesis analysis: In 69 advanced prostate cancer patients, 19% had CHIP variants, 10% in DNA repair genes; may lead to false PARP inhibitor eligibility; needs prospective validation.

What To Watch
