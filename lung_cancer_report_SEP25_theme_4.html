Small Cell Lung Cancer: Multiple Practice-Changing Advances in Brain Surveillance, Relapsed Disease, and Maintenance Therapy

"Several advances are reshaping small cell lung cancer (SCLC) [an aggressive, fast-growing lung cancer] management: the MAVERICK trial shows MRI surveillance alone [regular brain scans without preventive radiation] is safer for cognition than prophylactic cranial irradiation (PCI) [preventive whole-brain radiation] without harming survival; two B7-H3-targeted ADCs [antibody-drug conjugates, drugs that deliver chemotherapy directly to cancer cells] nearly doubled survival in relapsed SCLC; the UK approved lurbinectedin maintenance after first-line treatment; and a subcutaneous formulation of tarlatamab [an immune-activating drug given as an injection under the skin instead of IV] shows promising activity."

MAVERICK: 304 SCLC patients (limited/extensive stage) without brain mets after initial therapy randomized to MRI surveillance alone vs MRI + PCI. MRI alone significantly improved cognitive failure-free survival (HR 0.60, p=0.0005) with no OS detriment (HR 0.90). Grade 3+ toxicity: 0.8% vs 7.9%.
Relapsed SCLC: Two positive phase III trials vs topotecan [an older chemotherapy drug]: Risvutatug rezetecan (a B7-H3-targeted ADC [a drug that delivers chemotherapy to B7-H3-expressing cancer cells]) achieved median overall survival of 18.5 vs 10.3 months. Tambotatug pelitecan (another B7-H3 ADC) achieved median overall survival of 13.3 vs 9.4 months (HR 0.46 [a 54% reduction in risk of death]).
Maintenance: UK MHRA approved lurbinectedin + atezolizumab for ES-SCLC without progression after 1st-line atezolizumab + chemo. Median OS 13.2 vs 10.6 mo (HR 0.73), PFS 5.4 vs 2.1 mo (HR 0.54).
Tarlatamab (Imdelltra): A subcutaneous formulation is being studied (DeLLphi-308 trial). In relapsed extensive-stage SCLC, it showed a 30% tumor shrinkage rate with predominantly low-grade immune reactions [cytokine release syndrome, CRS — fever and flu-like symptoms]. The IV formulation is already FDA-approved for relapsed SCLC.
PCI omission: PCI causes permanent cognitive decline. MAVERICK proves structured MRI surveillance (q3mo x1yr, q6mo x1yr) is a safe, cognition-sparing alternative for many patients.
Relapsed SCLC: Topotecan has been the only 2nd-line option for decades with modest benefit (OS ~9-10 mo) and harsh toxicity. Two new B7-H3 ADCs nearly double survival with better tolerability.
Maintenance therapy: Extending disease control after first-line chemo-immunotherapy is a major unmet need. Lurbinectedin plus atezolizumab showed an overall survival benefit of 2.6 months (13.2 vs 10.6 months) and a progression-free survival [time before cancer worsens] benefit of 3.3 months (5.4 vs 2.1 months).
Subcutaneous tarlatamab: If validated, a shot under the skin every few weeks would be more convenient than IV infusions, potentially enabling treatment at community clinics rather than specialized centers (general medical guidance).
MAVERICK: Patients with limited or extensive-stage SCLC who complete 1st-line therapy (chemo ± immunotherapy) *without* brain metastases, willing to adhere to strict MRI schedule (q3mo x1yr, q6mo x1yr) and have access to rapid salvage SRS/WBRT if brain mets appear.
New ADCs (2nd-line+): Patients with relapsed ES-SCLC after platinum-based chemo ± immunotherapy. Both drugs target B7-H3 (broadly expressed on SCLC).
Lurbinectedin maintenance: Patients with ES-SCLC achieving disease control (CR/PR/SD) after 4-6 cycles of 1st-line atezolizumab + carboplatin + etoposide.
Tarlatamab SC: Patients with relapsed ES-SCLC seeking more convenient dosing (subcutaneous vs IV) with less CRS.
If completing 1st-line SCLC therapy without brain mets: Ask your radiation oncologist: "Based on MAVERICK, can I do MRI surveillance alone instead of PCI? Do we have a protocol for q3mo MRI and rapid SRS access?"
If relapsed after 1st-line: Ask "Am I eligible for a clinical trial with a B7-H3 ADC (risvutatug rezetecan or tambotatug pelitecan) or tarlatamab?" — these are not yet FDA-approved but have phase III data.
If in UK/ES-SCLC with disease control after 1st-line atezolizumab+chemo: Ask about lurbinectedin + atezolizumab maintenance (now approved).
Request brain MRI at baseline and per surveillance schedule — early detection of brain mets enables SRS instead of whole-brain radiation.

The Hope

  • Hope: MAVERICK changes default conversation — PCI no longer automatic for many SCLC patients.
  • Reality: Requires reliable MRI access and salvage SRS capacity — not available everywhere. OS not mature; PCI still reasonable if surveillance unreliable.
  • Hope: Two new ADCs offer first real improvement in 2nd-line SCLC in decades.

The Reality

  • Reality: ILD/pneumonitis risk requires vigilance. Access via trials or compassionate use only until approval. Cost unknown.
  • Hope: Maintenance therapy extends survival after 1st-line.
  • Reality: Adds 8 months median OS, but with monthly IV infusions and significant hematologic toxicity. Quality-of-life trade-off individual.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Risvutatug rezetecan: B7-H3-targeted ADC [a drug that delivers chemotherapy to cancer cells expressing B7-H3]. Positive phase III (ARTEMIS-008). FDA review anticipated.

Tambotatug pelitecan: B7-H3-targeted ADC. Positive phase III (TAISHAN-302, HR 0.46 for overall survival). NDA accepted for filing in China.

Lurbinectedin (Zepzelca): A DNA-damaging drug. UK MHRA approved for first-line maintenance with atezolizumab [an immune checkpoint inhibitor] in extensive-stage SCLC after initial chemo-immunotherapy (overall survival 13.2 vs 10.6 months, progression-free survival 5.4 vs 2.1 months).

Tarlatamab (Imdelltra): A DLL3-targeted BiTE [a drug that redirects immune T cells to kill DLL3-expressing cancer cells]. IV formulation FDA-approved for relapsed SCLC. Subcutaneous formulation being studied (DeLLphi-308).

Evidence Uncertainty

MAVERICK: Phase III with standardized cognitive testing; hippocampal-avoidance PCI used in 77% of the PCI arm. Limitations: Primary endpoint changed from overall survival to cognitive failure-free survival during the trial; overall survival data immature; some patients assigned to PCI did not complete it; cognitive testing available only in English and French.

B7-H3 ADCs: Strengths: Two independent phase III trials, large OS benefit (HR ~0.46), intracranial activity. Limitations: Higher interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis rates vs topotecan; not yet FDA-approved; long-term durability unknown.

Lurbinectedin maintenance: Strengths: Phase III, OS benefit. Limitations: Hematologic toxicity (grade 3-4 in 38%); UK-only approval so far; IMforte used atezolizumab induction (US standard often durvalumab).

What To Watch

FDA decisions on risvutatug rezetecan and tambotatug pelitecan (B7-H3 ADCs) for relapsed SCLC.

MAVERICK final overall survival analysis and full peer-reviewed publication.

US and European regulatory review for lurbinectedin plus atezolizumab maintenance.

DeLLphi-308 results: Extended-interval subcutaneous tarlatamab dosing for convenience.

Whether B7-H3 ADC and tarlatamab (DLL3-targeted) treatments can be sequenced or combined (general medical guidance).
