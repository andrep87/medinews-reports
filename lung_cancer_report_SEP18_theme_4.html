B7-H3 Antibody-Drug Conjugates Establish New Standard in Relapsed SCLC: Tam-Peli and Ris-Rez

"Two separate phase III trials in China—TAISHAN-302 (Tam-Peli) and ARTEMIS-008 (Ris-Rez)—showed that B7-H3–targeted antibody-drug conjugates [ADCs, antibodies linked to chemotherapy payloads] significantly extend overall survival versus topotecan chemotherapy in relapsed SCLC (Tam-Peli: 13.3 vs 9.4 months; Ris-Rez: 18.5 vs 10.3 months; both HR 0.46). This validates B7-H3 as a therapeutic target and positions ADCs to replace topotecan as second-line standard."

TAISHAN-302: 451 Chinese patients with relapsed SCLC after 1 prior platinum-based chemo ± PD-L1 inhibitor randomized to Tam-Peli (tambotatug pelitecan, 2 mg/kg IV day 1 q3w, max 200 mg) vs topotecan (1.2 mg/m² days 1–5 q3w). Median OS 13.3 vs 9.4 months (HR 0.46, p<0.0001). PFS 7.4 vs 2.8 months (HR 0.29). ORR 59.1% vs 9.7%. Benefit consistent in brain mets, liver mets, short chemo-free interval.
ARTEMIS-008: 461 patients (China) with similar population randomized to Ris-Rez (risvutatug rezetecan, 8 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks) vs topotecan. Median OS 18.5 vs 10.3 months (HR 0.46, p<0.0001). PFS 7.2 vs 3.0 months (HR 0.33). ORR 58.3% vs 12.6%. Disease control rate 90.4% vs 60.2%.
Tam-Peli targets B7-H3 [an immune checkpoint protein found on SCLC cells and tumor blood vessels, with low expression in healthy tissue] with a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload [chemotherapy that blocks DNA copying] via a TMALIN linker (dual release in tumor cell and surrounding environment). Ris-Rez also targets B7-H3; its specific payload mechanism was not detailed in the available sources.
Topotecan has been the default second-line SCLC option since 1996 with modest benefit (median OS ~9 months); no randomized trial had shown OS improvement over it until now.
Two independently developed B7-H3 ADCs from different companies (Roche/MediLink and GSK/Hansoh) both demonstrated 54% death risk reduction vs topotecan, validating the target and drug class.
ADCs deliver chemotherapy directly to tumor cells via antibody targeting, improving efficacy and reducing systemic toxicity compared to conventional chemo.
Tam-Peli showed intracranial activity: intracranial ORR 32.4% vs 2.9%, intracranial PFS 6.1 vs 4.2 months — important since SCLC frequently spreads to the brain. Ris-Rez intracranial data were not separately reported in the available sources.
Patients with relapsed SCLC (limited or extensive stage) after progression on first-line platinum-based chemotherapy ± immunotherapy (atezolizumab/durvalumab).
ECOG 0–1; measurable disease; prior PD-L1 inhibitor allowed (over 80% of ARTEMIS-008 patients had received one; TAISHAN-302 percentage not separately reported).
Patients with baseline brain metastases and liver metastases were included. Tam-Peli specifically showed consistent benefit in these subgroups; Ris-Rez sources state benefit was 'consistent across relevant subgroups' without specific brain/liver data.
Chemotherapy-free interval <90 days (platinum-resistant) also benefited.
If you have relapsed SCLC, ask: "Am I eligible for a B7-H3 ADC clinical trial (Tam-Peli or Ris-Rez) instead of topotecan?"
Discuss with your oncologist whether topotecan remains appropriate given these new data, or if a trial/access program is available.
Request brain MRI at progression to assess for CNS metastases, as both ADCs show intracranial activity.
Monitor for interstitial lung disease (ILD) [lung inflammation] symptoms (new/worsening cough, shortness of breath, fever) – Tam-Peli ILD rate was 4.9% (mostly low grade but can be serious). Ris-Rez ILD rate was not reported in the available sources.
Evidence Badges
Phase III Trial (TAISHAN-302) – Tam-Peli vs Topotecan Phase III Trial (ARTEMIS-008, NCT06498479) – Ris-Rez vs Topotecan China Breakthrough Therapy Designation (Tam-Peli); FDA Orphan Drug Designation status not confirmed in sources 8 Independent Sources (including NEJM publication for TAISHAN-302)

The Hope

  • Hope: Median OS 13–18 months vs 9–10 months with chemo; response rates ~60% vs 10%; potential to replace a 30-year-old chemo standard.
  • Reality: Tam-Peli NDA has been accepted for filing in China (not yet approved). Western access requires global trials (Roche has announced global phase III plans). Ris-Rez regulatory status outside China not specified in available sources.
  • IV administration every 3 weeks; infusion center visits required.

The Reality

  • Hematologic toxicity common (neutropenia, anemia, thrombocytopenia) but generally lower grade 3+ rates than topotecan.
  • ILD risk, while low grade in most, requires monitoring and may limit use in patients with pre-existing lung disease.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

Tam-Peli (Roche/MediLink): B7-H3 ADC with TMALIN linker (dual release in tumor cell and microenvironment); FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation; NDA accepted in China; global phase III trials planned.

Ris-Rez (risvutatug rezetecan, GSK/Hansoh): B7-H3 ADC with positive ARTEMIS-008 results. Global development plans not detailed in available sources.

Ifinatamab deruxtecan (Daiichi Sankyo/MSD): Another B7-H3 ADC under FDA priority review for extensive-stage SCLC (PDUFA Oct 10, 2026).

Tarlatamab (Imdelltra, Amgen): DLL3-targeted bispecific T-cell engager [redirects T cells to kill DLL3-expressing SCLC cells], approved for second-line SCLC (median OS 13.6 vs 8.3 months). Also being studied in first-line maintenance settings (general medical guidance).

Evidence Uncertainty

Strengths: Two large randomized phase III trials (451 and 461 patients) with consistent 54% OS HR reduction vs same control (topotecan); robust secondary endpoints (PFS, ORR, DCR); manageable safety (lower grade 3+ TRAEs vs topotecan: 46% vs 75% and 61% vs 78%).

Limitations: Both trials conducted exclusively in China (predominantly Asian, male, ever-smoker populations); generalizability to Western patients uncertain.

ILD/pneumonitis: Tam-Peli 4.9% vs 1.4% with topotecan. Ris-Rez ILD rate not reported in available sources — do not assume the same rate as Tam-Peli. ILD is a class effect of ADCs requiring monitoring.

No direct comparison between Tam-Peli and Ris-Rez; cross-trial comparisons invalid due to different patient populations and follow-up.

Sequencing with tarlatamab (DLL3 T-cell engager) undefined; both targets (B7-H3, DLL3) highly expressed in SCLC.

What To Watch

Global phase III trials for both ADCs outside China — Roche has announced plans for Tam-Peli global trials.

FDA decision on ifinatamab deruxtecan (PDUFA October 10, 2026) — a third B7-H3 ADC for SCLC.

Sequencing strategies between B7-H3 ADCs and tarlatamab (DLL3 T-cell engager) — both targets are expressed in SCLC but optimal order is unknown (general medical guidance).

Long-term safety, durability, and quality-of-life data from ongoing trial extensions.
