First Disease-Modifying Therapies Enter Human Testing with Novel Targets

"Three first-in-class disease-modifying candidates have advanced: TT-P34 (a lysosomal/mitochondrial enhancer) completed Phase 1, AB-300 (a non-hallucinogenic serotonin 2A agonist for apathy [loss of motivation]) is entering Phase 1b, and MTX325 (a mitophagy-restoring drug [boosts cleanup of damaged mitochondria]) is in Phase 1b. A fourth candidate, 1ST-104 (a dual LRRK2/c-Abl inhibitor), secured a $314.8 million licensing deal but remains preclinical. This marks a shift from symptom management toward targeting root biology."

TT-P34 (Teitur Trophics): Phase 1 trial (55 healthy, 12 early PD) showed weekly subcutaneous injection is safe, crosses blood-brain barrier [protective filter around brain], engages lysosomal [waste clearance] pathway in CSF, and supports weekly dosing; Phase 2 planned 2027.
1ST-104 (1ST Biotherapeutics/SK Biopharma): Preclinical oral dual inhibitor of LRRK2 [a kinase linked to genetic Parkinson's] and c-Abl [a kinase involved in alpha-synuclein clearance]; uses a Type 2 LRRK2 binding approach designed to avoid the lung toxicity seen with earlier Type 1 inhibitors; SK Biopharma acquired global license for $314.8 million. IND filing timeline has not been disclosed.
AB-300 (Ariadne Bio/Palomar Labs): Non-hallucinogenic serotonin 2A receptor agonist [activates motivation circuits without psychedelic effects]; restored effort-based behavior by 89% in dopamine-depleted rat model; MJFF-funded Phase 1b starting H2 2026 in Israel/Austria for apathy [loss of motivation, affects ~40% of PD patients].
MTX325 (Mission Therapeutics): USP30 inhibitor [blocks a brake on mitophagy, the mitochondrial cleanup process]; Phase 1b in PD patients reported as ongoing; the company has partnered with NeuraLight for AI eye-tracking to detect subtle brain function changes as a biomarker.
Current drugs (levodopa, dopamine agonists) only replace missing dopamine; they do not slow neuron loss. These four programs target distinct root mechanisms: protein clearance (TT-P34, 1ST-104), mitochondrial quality control (MTX325), and motivational circuitry (AB-300).
TT-P34’s dual lysosomal/mitochondrial action addresses two core drivers of neuron death simultaneously—unlike most single-target candidates.
1ST-104's Type 2 LRRK2 inhibition is designed to avoid the lung toxicity seen with earlier Type 1 inhibitors, while adding c-Abl inhibition to boost alpha-synuclein clearance. Earlier LRRK2 programs have had setbacks (general medical guidance).
AB-300 tackles apathy, a devastating non-motor symptom with zero approved treatments, distinct from depression (SSRIs often worsen apathy).
NeuraLight’s eye-tracking offers objective, scalable biomarker for early trials, replacing subjective clinician ratings.
TT-P34: Phase 2 will likely enroll early-stage PD patients — specific eligibility criteria not yet announced.
1ST-104: Future trials will likely focus on LRRK2 mutation carriers or patients with lysosomal dysfunction biomarkers; not yet recruiting.
AB-300: PD patients with clinically significant apathy — specific eligibility for Phase 1b not yet publicly detailed.
MTX325: PD patients — Phase 1b eligibility criteria not publicly disclosed.
Ask your neurologist: “Am I a candidate for any disease-modifying clinical trials based on my stage, genetics, or biomarkers?”
Get genetic testing (e.g., PD GENEration by MJFF, or commercial panel) to learn your LRRK2, GBA, SNCA status—this determines eligibility for 1ST-104 and other genotype-stratified trials.
If apathy is your main struggle (lack of drive, not sadness), discuss with your doctor whether AB-300 trials are recruiting near you; do not start SSRIs without screening for apathy vs. depression.
Register on Fox Trial Finder (foxtrialfinder.michaeljfox.org) and ClinicalTrials.gov alerts for “disease-modifying,” “LRRK2,” “apathy,” or “mitophagy” Parkinson’s studies.

The Hope

  • Hope: First time multiple distinct disease-modifying mechanisms (lysosomal, mitochondrial, LRRK2, motivation) are in clinic simultaneously—probability of at least one success higher than ever.
  • Reality: Phase 1/1b only prove safety/target engagement; Phase 2/3 for clinical benefit take 5–7 years; >90% of neurodegenerative candidates fail in Phase 2.

The Reality

  • Hope: AB-300 could deliver first-ever treatment for apathy, transforming quality of life for patients and caregivers.
  • Reality: Apathy overlaps with depression, cognitive decline, and medication side effects; isolating drug effect will be difficult; SSRIs are cheap/generic, new drug must show clear superiority.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

TT-P34 (Teitur Trophics): Peptide from SorCS2 receptor, activates CREB → lysosomal + mitochondrial genes; Phase 1 safe, CNS penetration confirmed; Series B fundraising for Phase 2 2027.

1ST-104 (1ST Biotherapeutics/SK Biopharma): Oral Type 2 LRRK2 + c-Abl inhibitor; preclinical; blood-brain barrier penetration and selectivity demonstrated in animal models; SK Life Science (US commercial arm) to lead development. IND filing timeline not disclosed.

AB-300 (Ariadne Bio): Serotonin 2A agonist, non-hallucinogenic; Phase 1b H2 2026 (Israel/Austria); MJFF Pipeline Program award; pre-IND Type B meeting completed 2026.

MTX325 (Mission Therapeutics): USP30 inhibitor, restores mitophagy; Phase Ia healthy volunteers showed brain penetration; Phase Ib PD ongoing (UK/EU/AU); NeuraLight oculometric [eye movement] endpoints.

Evidence Uncertainty

TT-P34: Phase 1 enrolled 67 participants (55 healthy volunteers, 12 early PD) in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study; primary endpoint was safety and pharmacokinetics; CSF lysosomal protein shifts are supportive biomarker signals but not clinical endpoints; weekly dosing is convenient but requires subcutaneous injection.

1ST-104: Zero human data yet; Type 2 LRRK2 differentiation unproven in humans; c-Abl inhibition may have off-target effects (c-Abl regulates DNA repair); Biogen/Denali Type 1 failure raises class concerns.

AB-300: Preclinical only (TBZ rat model); serotonin 2A agonism historically linked to hallucinations (though AB-300 claims non-hallucinogenic); apathy measurement in trials relies on caregiver-reported scales.

MTX325: Phase 1b design details not publicly disclosed; NeuraLight eye-tracking partnership reported but the biomarker's regulatory qualification status and correlation with clinical outcomes are unknown.

What To Watch

TT-P34 detailed Phase 1 data presentation at MDS Congress (Seoul, October 5, 2026); Phase 2 protocol design and site selection.

1ST-104: IND submission timeline not yet disclosed; watch for first-in-human study announcement.

AB-300 Phase 1b launch in H2 2026 (Israel/Austria); potential expansion to other apathy indications such as Alzheimer's disease.

MTX325 Phase 1b progress — design details and biomarker correlation data not yet publicly available.

PRISMS prevention trial recruitment pace and interim biomarker data.
