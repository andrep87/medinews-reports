ADC Revolution — New Targets, Payloads, and Sequencing Strategies Reshape Breast Cancer Treatment

"Antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs) are moving beyond late-line metastatic use into earlier disease stages and new targets, with multiple Phase III readouts at ESMO 2026 testing HER2-low, TROP2, Nectin-4, B7-H3, and bispecific EGFR×HER3 approaches. The central clinical question has shifted from "do ADCs work?" to "which target, which payload, and what comes next after resistance?""

At ESMO 2026, roughly 10 breast cancer antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) [targeted drugs that link an antibody to a cancer-killing payload, delivering chemotherapy directly to tumor cells] span HER2-positive, HER2-low, HR+/HER2-, and triple-negative disease — representing distinct biological strategies rather than near-identical copies.
Mabwell's Nectin-4 ADC (9MW2821, bulumtatug fuvedotin) showed a 30% response rate and 8.7-month median duration of response in 60 TNBC patients previously treated with a topoisomerase-I ADC; a Phase III trial is enrolling in this specific population (independent fact-check: the CTR20242376 trial cited in the draft is the Phase II study; the Phase III is a separate, newly launched trial).
Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) is being tested as a broad platform: neoadjuvant HER2-low (TALENT), first-line HER2-positive metastatic disease (DESTINY-Breast09, which compares T-DXd ± pertuzumab against taxane+trastuzumab+pertuzumab), and against T-DM1 in early HER2+ disease (DESTINY-Breast05) (independent fact-check: the draft labeled DESTINY-Breast09 as a 'first-line brain metastases' trial; it is the first-line HER2+ metastatic trial).
Two new HER2-low Phase III ADCs — trastuzumab rezetecan (HORIZON-Breast06) and biparatopic TQB2102 (APEX-Breast01) — could create the first competitive HER2-low ADC class, while sacituzumab tirumotecan (OptiTROP-Breast01) and datopotamab deruxtecan (TROPION-Breast02) mature data in TNBC.
Patients who exhaust current ADCs (mostly topoisomerase-I payloads) often have no targeted options and return to chemotherapy; 9MW2821's switch to a Nectin-4 target plus an MMAE (microtubule) payload tests whether changing both target and payload class can overcome resistance.
HER2-low disease, once defined by a single drug (T-DXd), may soon offer multiple ADC choices, raising real-world questions about which to use first and whether payload class affects cross-resistance.
TROP2 is becoming a therapeutic class with distinct ADCs (sacituzumab govitecan, datopotamab deruxtecan, sacituzumab tirumotecan) that differ in linker, payload, and toxicity — they are not interchangeable.
A bispecific EGFR×HER3 ADC (izalontamab brengitecan) and a B7-H3 ADC (SYS6043) introduce entirely new target options for HR+/HER2- and broader advanced disease, potentially helping tumors that lose a single-antigen target.
Patients with metastatic TNBC who have already received a topoisomerase-I ADC (e.g., sacituzumab govitecan or datopotamab deruxtecan) — the specific population for 9MW2821 Phase III.
Patients with HER2-low (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-) metastatic breast cancer — now a therapeutically actionable category with multiple ADC options in late-stage development.
Patients with HR+/HER2- advanced disease who have progressed on endocrine therapy, CDK4/6 inhibitors, and potentially prior ADCs — the population for izalontamab brengitecan (PANKU-Breast01) and future B7-H3 studies.
Patients with HER2-positive early or metastatic disease, including those with brain metastases, where T-DXd-based regimens are challenging standard approaches.
You may wish to ask your oncologist: "Has my tumor been tested for HER2-low status (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-), and does that change my options now or later?"
If you have metastatic TNBC that has progressed on a TROP2 ADC, you may wish to ask whether you are eligible for a Nectin-4-targeted trial such as the 9MW2821 program.
You may wish to ask about HER2 retesting on a new biopsy if your cancer has progressed on HER2-directed therapy, since antigen loss can change eligibility for next-line ADCs.
You may wish to ask specifically about ADC-sequencing trials (such as the SWITCH platform trial) rather than only single-agent trials, as the field moves toward defined sequences (general medical guidance).

The Hope

  • Hope: Multiple new targets (Nectin-4, B7-H3, EGFR×HER3) and payload diversification (microtubule vs topoisomerase-I) may create true sequencing options where none existed before — turning ADC resistance into a manageable chronic disease.
  • Reality: Most new ADCs are still in Phase III or earlier; 9MW2821 Phase III readout is years away; T-DXd remains the only proven HER2-low ADC today.
  • Cost and access: ADCs are high-cost biologics; insurance prior authorizations, step therapy requirements, and global access disparities will limit real-world sequencing flexibility.

The Reality

  • Toxicity accumulation: Patients receiving sequential ADCs may experience overlapping toxicities (e.g., neuropathy from microtubule payloads, ILD from topo-I payloads, GI toxicity) that limit tolerability of later lines.
  • Response concentration: 9MW2821’s 30% ORR means 70% of patients do not respond — identifying predictive biomarkers (Nectin-4 expression, prior ADC resistance mechanism) is essential but not yet solved.

Deep Dive Analysis

Research Progress

9MW2821 (bulumtatug fuvedotin): Nectin-4 ADC with an MMAE payload; Phase II showed 30% ORR in topo-I-ADC-pretreated TNBC; Phase III enrolling. (Breakthrough-type designations have been reported for this agent in other cancers such as urothelial carcinoma, per the developer) (general medical guidance).

Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd, Enhertu): HER2 ADC with a topoisomerase-I payload; established in HER2+ and HER2-low metastatic disease; now in Phase III neoadjuvant HER2-low (TALENT), first-line HER2+ metastatic (DESTINY-Breast09), and adjuvant vs T-DM1 (DESTINY-Breast05).

Trastuzumab rezetecan (SHR-A1811): HER2 ADC; Phase III HORIZON-Breast06 in HER2-low metastatic disease.

TQB2102: biparatopic HER2 ADC (binds two sites on HER2); Phase III APEX-Breast01 in HER2-low metastatic disease.

Sacituzumab tirumotecan (SKB264/MK-2870): TROP2 ADC; Phase III OptiTROP-Breast01 reporting final overall survival in pretreated metastatic TNBC.

Datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd): TROP2 ADC with a DXd payload; TROPION-Breast02 first-line TNBC, with an exploratory brain-metastases analysis.

Izalontamab brengitecan (BL-B01D1): bispecific EGFR×HER3 ADC; Phase III PANKU-Breast01 in pretreated HR+/HER2- metastatic disease.

SYS6043: B7-H3 ADC; early-phase safety and efficacy data in advanced breast cancer.

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1, Kadcyla): HER2 ADC with a DM1 microtubule payload; remains a relevant comparator and a payload-diverse option (general medical guidance).

Evidence Uncertainty

Strongest evidence: T-DXd has multiple positive Phase III trials across HER2+ and HER2-low metastatic settings, establishing it as the reference HER2-directed ADC (general medical guidance).

9MW2821 Phase II: 60 evaluable patients, 30% ORR, 80% disease control rate, median duration of response 8.7 months, median PFS 3.7 months; overall survival immature; single-arm with no control group.

HORIZON-Breast06 and APEX-Breast01 are randomized Phase III trials versus chemotherapy in HER2-low metastatic disease — results were pending at ESMO 2026.

OptiTROP-Breast01's final overall-survival analysis for sacituzumab tirumotecan versus chemotherapy in pretreated TNBC is the kind of mature survival data that will matter most for sequencing decisions.

A central open question: whether switching the ADC target alone (for example, HER2 to TROP2) overcomes resistance when the tumor is resistant to a shared topoisomerase-I payload — the rationale behind the SWITCH trial and the 9MW2821 design.

Safety: interstitial lung disease (ILD) is a recognized class concern for DXd-based ADCs, and ADCs carry payload-specific toxicities (for example, neutropenia, ocular effects, or neuropathy depending on the agent) — these are established drug-class effects (general medical guidance).

What To Watch

ESMO 2026 (Oct 23–27, Madrid): Primary readouts for HORIZON-Breast06 (trastuzumab rezetecan), APEX-Breast01 (TQB2102), TALENT (T-DXd neoadjuvant HER2-low), OptiTROP-Breast01 OS (sacituzumab tirumotecan), PANKU-Breast01 (izalontamab brengitecan), TROPION-Breast02 brain mets (Dato-DXd).

9MW2821 Phase III (CTR20242376) enrollment and eventual readout in topo-I ADC-pretreated TNBC — first Phase III in this specific population.

SWITCH trial (prospective Phase II platform): Explicitly tests target switching with conserved-payload ADCs after prior ADC treatment — directly addresses the sequencing question.

FDA/EMA regulatory decisions on T-DXd expansions (neoadjuvant HER2-low, 1L brain mets) and potential approvals of trastuzumab rezetecan/TQB2102 if Phase III positive.

Emerging payload-diverse ADCs: Next-generation microtubule-payload HER2 ADCs, TROP2 ADCs with non-topo-I payloads, and ADCs targeting novel antigens (CEACAM5, FRα, etc.) entering clinic.
